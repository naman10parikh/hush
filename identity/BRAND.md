# hush — BRAND

> Powered by Energy. Naming family: light / relatable / animate (Spark / Ribbon vibes — D4).

## Name

**hush.** A single, quiet word. The name *is* the product: a hush is the act of keeping something
quiet — and that is precisely the job. Keep the secret quiet: out of git, out of the context window,
out of the logs, out of the next commit. The name carries the whole doctrine in four letters.

It sits in the light / relatable / animate naming family the chairman locked for the C2 fleet (next to
pixel, pebble, turbo, acorn, badge, penny, rocket) — soft, human, one word, no enterprise-security
heaviness. A secrets tool that sounds calm is a feature: hush's whole personality is *calm and exact*,
the operator who refuses false-greens without raising their voice.

## Tagline

> **Issued, never leaked. Rotated, then proven.**

It encodes the one belief — *a leaked secret is not the failure; an un-rotated leak is* (brain `O-04`).
"Issued, never leaked" is prevention (keep it quiet); "Rotated, then proven" is remediation governance
(the actual product: rotate, then *prove* rotation).

## Symbolic connection to the workflow

| Brand element        | What it maps to in the work                                              |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| *hush* (keep quiet)  | The capability ladder — keep the value out of every channel it could leak through. |
| *Issued, never leaked* | JOB 1–2: prevention + scanning (`O-01`, `O-03`).                        |
| *Rotated, then proven* | JOB 3–4: rotate-first remediation, proven not assumed (`O-04`, `O-05`). |
| Calm voice           | Refusing the fast green checkmark — the false-green — every time (`P-06`). |

## Visual identity (cockpit)

The local cockpit follows the Energy design system: **warm-black surfaces (`#141312`, not pure black)**,
an **amber** accent, **Instrument Serif** for display + **Poppins** for body. No generic-purple / AI
gradients (`.claude/rules/design.md`). The cockpit is the chairman's eyes, not an agent surface.

## Landing copy (one line)

> *hush keeps your secrets out of git, your logs, and your agents' context — and tells you the real fix
> when one leaks: rotate first, prove it, then scrub. Powered by Energy.*
